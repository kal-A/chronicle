## Physical labels

The code-rendered labels use Natural Earth 1:50m marine-area naming as the spelling authority. `Caribbean Sea` is placed at 75°W, 15°N, within the central Caribbean basin; `North Atlantic Ocean` is placed at 35°W, 48°N. Labels are hidden rather than moved to an incorrect feature when the responsive crop becomes too constrained.
