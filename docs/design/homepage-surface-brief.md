## User Job

Begin a historical investigation immediately, understand that Chronicle will propose a bounded evidence-backed scope, and continue into the map workspace without feeling redirected into another product.

## First Viewport

The viewport contains the Chronicle wordmark, minimal navigation, one dominant historical question field, three compact investigation starters, and only a thin glimpse of the Ask → Review scope → Explore sequence. The map field supplies depth but remains subordinate to the question.

## Signature Interaction

The homepage atlas behaves like a palimpsest while the user types. After scope approval and generation—not immediately on initial submit—the atlas dissolves into cartographic particles that resolve into the investigation's bounded map. The question surface persists as a shared element and becomes the workspace assistant.

## Accuracy Gate

No generated raster map may ship as the geographic base. The first visible coastline must come from sourced geometry. Period scans and annotations remain distinct provenance-bearing layers; unsupported borders, routes, and labels are omitted. An unavailable historical layer produces a disclosed neutral map or schematic, never decorative invention.

The full acceptance checklist is `docs/design/cartographic-quality-gate.md`. It covers spelling, period-valid names, label placement, geographic precision, projection, scale, zoom limits, and transition fidelity.

## Scope Boundary

This round covers the homepage/Ask surface, its compact workflow glimpse, and the transition into the existing map-first workspace. It does not redesign every investigation tool or add professional-historian workflows.
