# July Crisis Source Register — Initial Phase 0 Draft

This register is the initial research map for the July Crisis investigation. It is not a declaration that the listed material has passed Chronicle's human review bar. `curated` means that the source's relevance and limitations have been recorded; it does not mean that any derived claim is `reviewed`.

Links identify an accessible copy, catalogue record, or publisher record. Passage extraction must record the exact edition/translation used.

## Coverage and Acquisition Gaps

- French and Russian internal decision records require stronger original-language documentary coverage before Phase 2 seed content can be reviewed.
- The Sarajevo investigation and Serbian government/Black Hand relationship need dedicated primary and specialist sources; the current register is stronger from 5 July onward.
- Exact passage extraction, edition metadata, and translation comparison remain outstanding for every source except `jc-src-001`, `jc-src-002`, `jc-src-020`, and `jc-src-021` (extracted for Scene 2, see `docs/research/scene-2-content-fixture.md`).
- `jc-src-016` (Fischer) and `jc-src-013` (Clark) remain `identified` only — Scene 2's disputed-relationship claim currently cites `jc-src-020`'s characterization of both positions rather than direct passages from either book. Acquiring both directly for passage-level citation remains an open gap.
- Received-time (as opposed to sent-time) for both Scene 2 telegrams is not yet established from the cited editions — recorded as an explicit gap in the content fixture rather than assumed.
- No Claim, Relationship, or KnownAtTime record may be marked `reviewed` merely because its parent source appears in this register.
